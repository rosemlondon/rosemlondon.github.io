arts council grant


Overwhelmingly happy to say that I was awarded the Arts Council NLPG grant I applied for in December!!!!

I've felt that blacksmithing is the next step for my arts practice for a while, but if you're someone like me who does not have any spare cash... getting into blacksmithing can be really tricky. There's a lot of financial hurdles, especially at the outset.
There's essentially one 3 year degree in Hereford you can take - and the alternative is training directly with existing smiths, which I'd much rather do. Putting my money directly into the pockets of metalworkers is more up my street. This grant covers a year of training with mostly female blacksmiths - but also allows me to work a bit less at my job, to spend time on this training.
This blog is a really important part of my project, too. I want to have a space to easily update and share all the knowledge that I’m learning. So this might be quite technical, or very specific to learning to forge, but I hope in the future it serves as an archive, for more women (or anyone!) getting into the craft.
More updates to follow!
