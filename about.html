myrtle cross


myrtle cross is the art pseudonym I chose four years ago and it has stuck.

I'm Rose, I'm a multidisciplinary artist, writer, gardener and engineer.

I make collographs from industrial materials to mimic surfaces of tools, and forge the tools themselves in steel. The same objects in ink and in metal. I write about rhizomes, technology, the female body, and Manchester, the city where I live. i'm interested in the continuity between making disciplines — the way knowledge lives in the hand rather than on the page.

I am currently undertaking forging fragments, a practice-led research project supported by arts council england, developing my forging skills and the history of hand tools in print.

i am not available for commissions at the moment.
